Privacy policy

This application stores the financial records you enter in its configured PostgreSQL database. It does not include account registration or user authentication. Anyone with access to the running application and its database may be able to view or change records.

Currency requests

When you use currency conversion, the backend sends the requested currency codes to Frankfurter to retrieve published exchange-rate data. No financial transaction details are sent to that service. Currency rates are cached in application memory for up to six hours.

Storage and retention

Transactions, budgets, and activity entries remain in the database until they are deleted or the database is removed by its operator. Database credentials are configured by the operator and should not be committed to source control.

Contact and updates

This is a self-hosted starter project with no operator contact service. The person deploying it is responsible for providing an appropriate contact and updating this policy for their use and jurisdiction.
